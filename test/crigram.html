Install CRIGram

Put CRIGram on your home screen. It opens full screen and also works offline.

Open CRIGram in your browser first

On iPhone or iPad

  1. Tap ⋯ next to the address bar or on three horizontal stripes in the address bar, then Share. If you see the Share button itself, tap that. Tap the Share button in the address bar or in the menu.
  2. Tap View More (or scroll down), then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add at the top right. If you see Open as Web App, leave it on.

The app starts with an empty album: on iPhone and iPad an installed app keeps its own progress, separate from Safari.

On Android

  1. Tap the menu ⋮ at the top right.
  2. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen, then Install.
  1. In the menu bar, choose File › Add to Dock.
  2. Click Add. CRIGram then opens from the Dock in its own window.

The app starts with an empty album: an app in the Dock keeps its own progress, separate from Safari.

    How to play

    Each puzzle hides part of a figure from a CRIG paper.
    • Each number is a run: a group of filled cells right next to each other. The numbers next to a row give its runs from left to right; the numbers above a column give its runs from top to bottom.
      1 4 1
      For example, 1 4 1 means: a run of 1, then a run of 4, then a run of 1. Here the run of 4 even crosses a thick line.
    • Between two runs there is at least one empty cell; how many, and where the runs start, is up to you to work out. A 0 means the whole line is empty.
    • The thick lines every 5 cells only help you count. They have nothing to do with the numbers.
    • Use Fill to color cells and Blank to turn cells white when you know they stay empty. Gray cells are still undecided.
    • Drag along a row or column to mark several cells at once. Tap a marked cell again to clear it.
    • Every puzzle can be solved by logic alone. No guessing needed!
    • Cells too small? Use + and − to zoom. Drag along the clue numbers (or with two fingers) to move around; the mini-map in the corner shows where you are.
    • Stuck? The bulb gives you a hint.
    • Stars show how hard a puzzle is: ★☆☆☆ easy (a typical player needs less than 5 minutes), ★★☆☆ medium (5 to 10 minutes), ★★★☆ hard (10 to 20 minutes) and ★★★★ expert (more than 20 minutes). There is no clock while you play: take your time. You see your time once you have solved the puzzle.
    • A new puzzle appears every Monday at 06:00 (Brussels time), or occasionally on special dates. Solve the Monday puzzle within the week to keep your streak going; special puzzles are a bonus and never break your streak. Tap Weekly reminder to put a Monday reminder in your calendar.

    CRIGram counts anonymous plays to see which puzzles are popular. No cookies, no personal data.
