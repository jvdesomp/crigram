Install CRIGram

Put CRIGram on your home screen. It opens full screen and also works offline.

iPhone or iPad (Safari): tap the Share button ⎋ at the bottom of the screen, then Add to Home Screen.

Android (Chrome): tap the menu ⋮ at the top right, then Install app or Add to Home screen.

How to play

Each puzzle hides part of a figure from a CRIG paper.
  • The numbers next to each row and above each column tell you the lengths of the filled blocks in that line, in order.
  • Between two blocks there is at least one empty cell. A 0 means the line is empty.
  • Use Fill to colour cells and Blank to turn cells white when you know they stay empty. Grey cells are still undecided.
  • Drag along a row or column to mark several cells at once. Tap a marked cell again to clear it.
  • Every puzzle can be solved by logic alone. No guessing needed!
  • Stuck? The bulb gives you a hint.
  • A new puzzle appears every Monday at 06:00 (Brussels time). Solve it within the week to keep your streak going. Tap Weekly reminder to put a Monday reminder in your calendar.

CRIGram counts anonymous plays to see which puzzles are popular. No cookies, no personal data.
